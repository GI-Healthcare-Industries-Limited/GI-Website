# Supporter artwork

Support relationships were supplied by the owner in their pitch deck. Artwork is kept in its original colours; all links identify the organisation/programme, not an investment or endorsement claim.

Official website sources: Ministry of Defence (gov.uk), Dstl (gov.uk), Innovate UK and STFC (ukri.org), Barclays Eagle Labs (labs.uk.barclays), UK Space Agency Accelerator (ukspaceaccelerator.co.uk), University of Bristol (bristol.ac.uk), Heriot-Watt University (hw.ac.uk), UWE Bristol (uwe.ac.uk), University of Glasgow (gla.ac.uk), Nexus Creative (nexuscreativehq.com). MOD and Heriot-Watt artwork was already used on the existing GI homepage. Other source artwork was downloaded during the approved concept exploration.

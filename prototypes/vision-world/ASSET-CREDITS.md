# Asset provenance — realism refinement

No third-party executable Blender files were loaded. Geometry is original and reproducible from `scripts/build-world.py`. The native source and GLBs are local-only.

## Poly Haven — CC0

All maps below are supplied under [Poly Haven's CC0 licence](https://polyhaven.com/license). Source links, download URLs and byte sizes are recorded in `public/assets/materials/sources.json`.

- [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock): grass colour/normal, colour-tuned in the web renderer.
- [Concrete Wall 006](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_wall_006): fine normal detail; painted surfaces retain GI's ivory palette.
- [Coast Sand Rocks 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_rocks_02): geological cutaway surfaces.
- [Wood Planks Dirt](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks_dirt): trunk texture. The initial rustic furniture treatment was replaced during QA.
- [Oak Veneer 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01), Jenelle van Heerden: clean oak furniture/floor surfaces.
- [Tree Small 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02), Rico Cilliers: leaf colour/alpha atlas only. Branches and tree layouts are original; no high-polygon tree model was imported.
- [Overcast Soil](https://polyhaven.com/a/overcast_soil): 1K photographic HDR environment.

Images are resized for web delivery, UV-mapped, and combined into local GLB textures. Original downloads remain beside generated proxies.

## Planetary imagery

- Moon: [NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio — CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720), Ernie Wright; Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter/LROC data. Uses `lroc_color_2k.jpg` on original spherical geometry.
- Mars: [Solar System Scope / INOVE](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), **[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**. Uses `2k_mars.jpg`, mapped, lit and tone-mapped. Credit also appears in the preview's linked credits page. No endorsement is implied.

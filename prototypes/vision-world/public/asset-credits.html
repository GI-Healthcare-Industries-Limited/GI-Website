Asset credits

This local concept combines original Blender geometry with licensed photographic material maps. It illustrates GI Healthcare's vision, not existing certified deployments.

  • Surface maps and daylight environment from Poly Haven, under CC0: Aerial Grass Rock, Concrete Wall 006, Coast Sand Rocks 02, Wood Planks Dirt, Oak Veneer 01, Overcast Soil, and the leaf atlas from Tree Small 02 (Rico Cilliers). Maps are resized, tiled and colour-tuned. Only the leaf texture is used; the tree geometry is original.
  • Moon colour map: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio — CGI Moon Kit, Ernie Wright; LRO/LROC data. Mapped onto original spherical geometry.
  • Mars texture: Solar System Scope / INOVE, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Mapped, lit and tone-mapped in the scene. This credit does not imply endorsement.

GI branding, reference artwork and supporter logos were supplied by GI Healthcare or taken from its existing website.
